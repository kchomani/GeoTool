Privacy policy

Geo Tool for Windows, including the Microsoft Store version. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Geo Tool is a free program made by Kaifi Chomani (Egeocate Lab for GIS & Remote Sensing). It has no account or sign-in, shows no advertising, and does not track you. Geo Tool itself does not collect, store or send any personal information to its author.

What Geo Tool sends over the internet

To make a map, Geo Tool downloads free open data for the area or point you choose. To do that it sends the coordinates of your area or point, the dates you pick and the program's name and version to the public services that hold the data:

  • Microsoft Planetary Computer: satellite images (Sentinel-2, Landsat, MODIS), elevation models and land cover
  • Esri and EOX: the satellite background map and place names
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass servers) and Overture Maps: buildings, roads, places and water
  • Open-Meteo (ERA5 rainfall), ISRIC SoilGrids (soil), the Climate Hazards Center at UC Santa Barbara (CHIRPS rainfall)
  • NASA FIRMS (active fires), earthquake catalogues, and GitHub (the active-fault database)

As with any web request, these services also see your computer's internet (IP) address. Each one handles that under its own privacy policy. Geo Tool sends nothing else: not your name, files, drawings or results.

What stays on your computer

  • Maps, tables, GeoTIFFs and Shapefiles are saved in the folder you choose (the Desktop unless you pick another).
  • Downloaded map tiles and data are kept in a working folder on your computer, so they are not downloaded again. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version removes them; with the zip version, delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\GeoTool.
  • Files you open in Geo Tool (areas, points, spreadsheets) are read on your computer and are not uploaded.

Children

Geo Tool is an educational program and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
